Let's see DEFY in practice.
When an idea is born, the plan stays fixed on the signal candle. Entry can be on the candle close or on a fifty percent pullback.
Right after it comes the Warning: if price gets there, the idea is losing strength.
The stop sits one ATR beyond the Warning.
And there are three targets: one R, two R and three R, or the Squeeze, Stretch and Swing modes. The distance shows in pips on forex and in points on gold, indices and crypto.
As the trade moves, DEFY marks the follow-up: entry hit, TP1, TP2, TP3 or stop. And the alerts arrive with all of it.
Always set your risk before you enter, and practise on a demo account first.
