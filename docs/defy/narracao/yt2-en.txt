Confirmations are the heart of DEFY.
Every idea starts with price stretching into the bands and turning with a strong candle. Then DEFY counts what is in its favour.
Is price at a key level? One more confirmation. Is the trend cloud on the same side? One more. Is there a volume break? One more.
With three confirmations the idea shows one star. With more, two or three stars.
And when everything lines up, the candle turns gold: that is the King Idea, the idea with the most confirmations.
Remember: stars measure how many confirmations there are, they do not guarantee the outcome. Risk management is still yours.
