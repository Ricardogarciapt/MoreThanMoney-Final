What is DEFY? It is a TradingView indicator that brings four readings onto one chart.
First, the exhaustion bands: inner, mid and outer, around a smooth average, with a dashed projection forward.
Second, key levels: zones where price has reacted before, spaced apart so the chart stays clean.
Third, the trend cloud, which tells you which way the market is pulling.
And fourth, the confirmation score, from plus three to plus fifteen, with stars.
The idea is simple: price stretches into the bands, turns with a strong candle and, if there are enough confirmations, DEFY marks the idea and draws the full plan.
