# From demo to real

> A real account does not teach you anything new. It just shows you, with money, what you have not yet learnt on the demo.

There is a rush almost everyone feels at the end of two weeks on a demo: "I've got it now, I want to do this for real." Two weeks on a demo is about ten trades. Ten trades say nothing about you, about the plan or about the market. They only say that you made ten trades.

Moving to a real account is not a promotion for good behaviour. It is a test with criteria.

:::alerta Classic mistake · Going live too early
The demo went well for a week and the urge to "win for real" takes over. The real account opens with a bigger deposit than planned, the lot goes up "because now it's real", and the emotions that did not exist on the demo all arrive on the same day. The demo is there so you can make cheap mistakes. Leave it when your numbers show you are ready, not when the urge says so.
:::

## The criteria for moving on

You only move to a real account when you have met **all** of these:

1. **At least 40 trades** on the demo with the same written plan.
2. **Plan adherence of 90% or more** over the last 20.
3. **Zero trades without a stop** across the whole period.
4. **A complete journal**, with a screenshot before and after every trade.
5. **You respected the stopping rules** every time they were triggered.
6. **Average win greater than average loss** over the last 20 (before you give any weight to the win rate).

Notice that none of the criteria is "I made X euros". A demo with a profit and 60% adherence is a worse sign than a slightly negative demo with 95% adherence. The first one had luck. The second one has a process.

:::cartao Criteria to go live
- 40 trades with the same plan
- Adherence ≥ 90% over the last 20
- Zero trades without a stop
- Complete journal
- Stopping rules respected
- Average win > average loss
:::

## How to start on a real account

1. **Deposit only what you are prepared to lose** without touching your income, your bills or your emergency fund. If you do not yet have an emergency fund, "I Destroyed It All. And I'm Glad." has a whole chapter on it, and it comes before trading.
2. **Start with the minimum lot** for the first 20 real trades, even if the 1% formula allows more. The first phase of a real account is for getting your nervous system used to it, not your wallet.
3. **Keep exactly the same plan** as on the demo. Changing the plan when you change the account means throwing away everything you have trained.
4. **Compare adherence week by week** on the demo and on the real account. If it drops, go back to the demo. That is not a step backwards. It is maintenance.

:::destaque
The first real account is not there to make money. It is there to prove to yourself that you follow the plan when the money is yours.
:::
