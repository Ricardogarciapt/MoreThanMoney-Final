# The market does not know you exist

> When you buy euros against dollars, there is someone on the other side convinced of the opposite. At least one of you is wrong.

You go to London on holiday and exchange €500 at the airport. You get pounds, pay a fee you do not really understand and carry on with your trip. Without noticing, you have just taken part in the biggest market in the world. Forex is exactly this: swapping one currency for another. The difference is that the tourist exchanges because they need pounds, and the trader exchanges because they think the price is going to move.

According to the 2022 survey by the Bank for International Settlements, the foreign exchange market turns over around 7.5 trillion dollars a day. Central banks, commercial banks, companies paying suppliers abroad, funds and, at the very end of the queue, us: small traders with an app on their phone.

This number serves one purpose only: to understand that your order of 0.01 lots does not move anything. The market does not know you exist. It is not going to "hunt your stop" because it is you, and it is not going to get back at you for watching. This sounds cold, and it is. It is also liberating: you stop taking the market personally.

## Pairs

In Forex you always trade in pairs. EUR/USD at 1.0850 means that 1 euro is worth 1.0850 dollars.

- The first currency is the **base** (EUR).
- The second is the **quote** (USD).
- **Buying** EUR/USD is betting that the euro will rise against the dollar.
- **Selling** EUR/USD is betting on the opposite. And yes, you can sell without owning euros: you are trading a contract for difference (CFD) on the price change, not buying banknotes.

## On the screen: your first reading of Quotes

Open the app you installed in the previous chapter and tap **Quotes**. This is where the market enters your phone.

:::galeria Each line is a pair
mt5-tel-cotacoes.png | Sell on the left, buy on the right. Underneath, the day's low (L) and high (H).
:::

On each line you have:

- **The pair** (EURUSD, GBPCAD…) and, above it, the day's change in points and in percent.
- **The time of the last price** and, next to it, the **spread** in points.
- **Two large prices.** The one on the left is the price at which you sell (*bid*) and the one on the right is the price at which you buy (*ask*).
- **L and H**: the day's low and high.

For example, EUR/USD at 1.08502 / 1.08510 (illustrative values). The difference between the two is the **spread**, and it is the first thing you pay on every trade: here, 0.8 pips. In the next chapter you will see what that is worth in money.

:::cartao Red does not mean losing
The colours only tell you whether the last price went down (red) or up (blue) compared with the previous one. They have nothing to do with your account.
:::

If you tap a pair, a menu opens: **New order**, **Chart**, **Specification** and other options. For now, just look. Orders have their own chapter.

### Adding and tidying up pairs

:::galeria Search by name
mt5-tel-procurar-simbolo.png | Tap the "+" in Quotes and type the pair's name. Assets are grouped: Forex, commodities, indices and crypto.
:::

1. In **Quotes**, tap the **+** and type the name of the pair (for example, EURUSD).
2. Tap the result to add it to the list.
3. To remove a pair or change the order, tap the pencil icon: drag to reorder and delete the ones you do not use.
4. Keep the pairs in your plan at the top.

Keep the list short. The pairs in your plan and not much else. A list with forty lines invites you to hunt for opportunities instead of waiting for your own.

| Group | Examples | What you should know |
|---|---|---|
| Majors | EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY | Lowest spreads, most liquidity, the best ones to start with |
| Crosses | EUR/GBP, EUR/JPY, GBP/JPY | No dollar; some move a lot |
| Exotics | USD/TRY, EUR/ZAR | Wide spreads and sudden jumps: stay away at the start |
| Gold | XAU/USD | Not a currency, but it trades alongside; volatile |

:::historia Marta, day 2
The first thing Marta did was fill up Quotes: forty-two symbols, from gold to oil, from bitcoin to the Turkish lira. For an hour, the red and blue flashing looked like opportunities slipping away. At the end of her shift she deleted everything and kept three lines: EURUSD, GBPUSD and, just to watch, XAUUSD. The screen became boring. That was exactly what she wanted.
:::

:::alerta Classic mistake · Starting with the most "exciting" pair
Those starting out watch a video with gold or GBP/JPY making huge moves and immediately want that one. It is like passing your driving test and going straight onto the motorway at rush hour. Pairs that move a lot punish sizing and stop mistakes far more. Start with a major, with a low spread, and learn the rule before the adrenaline.
:::

## When the market is awake

Forex runs 24 hours a day, five days a week, because the day moves from one financial centre to the next: Sydney, Tokyo, London, New York. But not every hour is the same.

[[grafico:sessoes-forex]]

For those living in Portugal, there is good news: the window with the most movement in the euro and dollar pairs, when London and New York are open at the same time, falls roughly between 1:00 PM and 5:00 PM (Lisbon time). The bad news is that it is the middle of the afternoon, when most of us are at work. That is why, in chapter 7, you will choose a short, fixed window instead of checking your phone all day.

:::nota
A market open 24 hours does not mean "I have to watch it 24 hours a day". It means I choose my hour and ignore the others.
:::

## Who is on the other side

When you open a trade with a retail broker, the broker is your counterparty or passes your order on to liquidity providers. For you, in practice, this means three things:

1. **Choose a regulated broker** and check on the regulator's website that the entity you open the account with is really the one it claims to be. In Portugal, the CMVM publishes warnings about unauthorised entities.
2. **Read the risk information document** the broker gives you. The percentage of retail accounts that lose money is in there.
3. **Your account is yours.** At MoreThanMoney, the broker route we use is PU Prime, and the account always stays in your name, with the broker. Never hand your money to a person to "manage it for you".

:::conversa WhatsApp · Unknown number
Ana: Hi! I saw you're interested in Forex 😊 I manage accounts, 15% a month guaranteed. Send me €500 and I'll handle everything.
tu: Guaranteed?
Ana: Yes, I have a robot that never loses.
tu: Blocked.
:::

If it has not happened to you yet, it will. Nobody reputable guarantees returns in Forex. Nobody reputable asks you for money to put into their account.

When one day you move from the demo to a real account (and you only do that in chapter 11), ask the broker these four questions:

1. **Who regulates me?** Look up the entity and the licence number on the regulator's website, not only on the broker's website.
2. **Which entity holds my account?** Many groups have several entities in different countries, with different rules.
3. **What does it cost?** Typical spread, commissions, swap, withdrawal costs.
4. **Can I withdraw?** Read the withdrawal rules before you deposit.

## The cold rule of this chapter

:::cartao Cold rule no. 2
The market does not know I exist. I do not take anything personally, I do not look for revenge, I do not expect fairness. I choose the pair, I choose the hour, I follow the rule.
:::

## In short

- Forex is swapping one currency for another; the trader swaps because they think the price is going to move.
- Every price has two sides, buy and sell, and the difference is the spread.
- Start with a major pair. Exotics and gold come later.
- In Quotes, sell on the left, buy on the right; the short list is your list.
- The most active window for us, in Portugal, runs from about 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM.
- A regulated broker, an account in your name, and never money handed to third parties.
