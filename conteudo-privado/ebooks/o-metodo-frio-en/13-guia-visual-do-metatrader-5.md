# A visual guide to MetaTrader 5

> Keep these pages near your phone. The first time, tap slowly.

Throughout the book we talked about MetaTrader 5 in bits: the account in chapter 3, the order in 4, the chart in 5, the news in 7. Here it is all together, sorted by theme, with real screenshots of the app on a phone. This is the page you open when you cannot remember where something is.

Account details are blurred on purpose. The prices you see are from an ordinary day and are only there so you recognise the screens (illustrative example).

## 1. Getting in and finding your way

When you open the app for the first time there are five icons at the bottom: quotes, chart, trade, history and settings. Always start with settings, to check you are on the right account (the demo, while you practise).

:::nota Before you tap "Buy"
Check the account name at the top of settings.
If it says "Real" and you are still practising, stop.

## 2. Reading the chart

The chart opens on the pair you picked in quotes. At the top you have the timeframe (M1, H1, D1…), the crosshair, indicators and drawing objects. To start with, candles and H1 are enough.

:::galeria Same phone, two timeframes
mt5-video-grafico.png | M1: one candle per minute. Too much noise for a beginner.
mt5-video-grafico-velas.png | H1: one candle per hour. This is where you work.
:::

Look at the coloured label on the right: it is the current price, with a countdown until the candle closes. If it has not closed, it has not happened yet.

[[foto: mt5-analise-tecnica.jpg | On a computer you can fit several charts and timeframes side by side. Image: MetaQuotes (metatrader5.com). | larga]]

## 4. News and calendar

News and the economic calendar live inside the platform. On the phone they are in settings ("News" and "Tradays"). On a computer they are in the toolbox at the bottom.

[[foto: mt5-calendario-noticias.jpg | "News" and "Calendar" tabs in MetaTrader 5 for desktop. Image: MetaQuotes (metatrader5.com). | larga]]

## Quick map

| You want to… | Tap… |
|---|---|
| See a pair's price | **Quotes**, the first icon at the bottom |
| Open the chart | Tap the pair and choose **Chart** |
| Change the timeframe | The **H1** button in the corner of the chart |
| Open an order | The **+** icon or **Trade › New order** |
| See balance and margin | **Trade** |
| See closed trades | **History** |
| News and calendar | **Settings › News / Tradays** |

:::exercicio To do today
1. Open MetaTrader 5 on the demo account and check the account name in settings.
2. Put EURUSD on candles, H1.
3. Prepare a pending order with an S/L, but do not confirm it: just look at the line on the chart.
4. Cancel it, and write in your journal how much you would lose if the stop were hit.
:::
