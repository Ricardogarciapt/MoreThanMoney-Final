# The 30-day plan and glossary

> Thirty days will not make you a trader. They will make you someone who follows a plan for thirty days, and that is rarer than it sounds.

This plan is done entirely on a demo account, with 30 to 45 minutes a day. It does not skip steps and it does not speed up. If you miss a day, do not make it up the next day with double the work: carry on from where you left off.

## Week 1 · Getting to know the ground

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| 1 | Install MT5, open a demo with a realistic balance, choose your pair (chapters 1 and 3) |
| 2 | Spreads at three different times of day; symbol specification (chapter 2) |
| 3 | Clean chart: candles, H1 and H4, no indicators. First screenshot for the journal |
| 4 | Trend on the H4: write up, down or sideways, with the reason (chapter 5) |
| 5 | Draw two support zones and two resistance zones |
| 6 | Economic calendar for the following week; set your window (chapter 7) |
| 7 | Rest. Re-read chapters 0 to 3 |

## Week 2 · Training your fingers

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| 8 | Open and cancel pending orders with SL and TP (chapter 4) |
| 9 | Work out the lot for three different stops, with proof (chapter 6) |
| 10 | Write the plan on the one-page card (chapter 8) |
| 11 | Create the journal with all the columns (chapter 9) |
| 12 | First trade with a complete plan and checklist |
| 13 | Second trade, or none if there is no setup |
| 14 | First Sunday review |

## Week 3 · Repeat without decoration

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| 15 to 20 | Trade only in your window, and only with a setup from the plan. Journal on the same day, emotional state before and after |
| 21 | Sunday review: adherence, losses with and without the plan, one change |

## Week 4 · Looking at yourself in the mirror

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| 22 to 27 | Carry on. Activate the stopping rules whenever they apply, with no exceptions (chapter 10) |
| 28 | Work out the three numbers: adherence, win rate, average win ÷ average loss |
| 29 | Compare with the criteria for moving on (chapter 11) |
| 30 | Write a letter to yourself: what you learnt, what you will keep, what you will change |

## Glossary

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Pip** | The unit of price movement; the fourth decimal place on most pairs |
| **Lot** | The size of the position: 1.00 (standard), 0.10 (mini), 0.01 (micro) |
| **Spread** | The difference between the buy price (ask) and the sell price (bid); the cost of entry |
| **Swap** | The interest paid or received for holding a position open overnight |
| **Stop-loss (SL)** | The price at which the trade closes by itself if it goes wrong |
| **Take-profit (TP)** | The price at which the trade closes by itself if it goes right |
| **Margin** | The money the broker sets aside to keep a position open |
| **Leverage** | Controlling a position bigger than your capital; increases both gains and losses |
| **Margin call / stop out** | Warning and automatic closing when there is not enough margin |
| **Equity** | The balance plus or minus the result of open positions |
| **Drawdown** | The fall in the account from its highest point to its lowest |
| **Breakeven** | The point at which the trade neither gains nor loses |
| **Limit order** | Buys below or sells above the current price |
| **Stop order** | Buys above or sells below the current price, on a breakout |
| **Slippage** | The difference between the price requested and the price executed |
| **Timeframe** | The period each candle covers: M15, H1, H4, D1 |
| **Support / resistance** | Zones where the price has stopped falling / rising |
| **Technical analysis** | Reading the price and the chart |
| **Fundamental analysis** | Reading the economy: interest rates, inflation, employment |
| **CFD** | Contract for difference: you trade the price movement without owning the asset |
