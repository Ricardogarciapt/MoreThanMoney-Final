# 30-day plan and glossary

> Thirty days will not make you a trader. They will make you someone who follows a plan for thirty days, and that is rarer than it sounds.

This plan is done entirely on a demo account, with 30 to 45 minutes a day. It does not skip steps and it does not speed up. If you miss a day, do not make up for it the next day with double; carry on from where you stopped.

## Week 1 · Getting to know the ground

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| 1 | Install MT5 on your phone and PC, open a demo with a realistic balance, set up the chart (chapter 1) |
| 2 | Quotes: short list, choose the pair, spreads at three different times (chapter 2) |
| 3 | Symbol specification and the "Volume" field: what a pip is worth (chapter 3) |
| 4 | Chart: candles, timeframes, trend on H4 with the reason (chapter 4) |
| 5 | Draw two support zones and two resistance zones with the app's objects. First screenshot for the journal |
| 6 | Calendar (Tradays) for the coming week; define your window; first price alert (chapter 7) |
| 7 | Rest. Reread chapters 0 to 4 |

## Week 2 · Training your fingers

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| 8 | Calculate the lot for three different stops, with proof (chapter 5) |
| 9 | Open, modify and cancel pending orders with SL and TP, confirming in the money view (chapter 6) |
| 10 | Write the plan on the one-page card (chapter 8) |
| 11 | Create the journal with all the columns (chapter 9) |
| 12 | First trade with the full plan and checklist |
| 13 | Second trade, or none if there is no setup |
| 14 | First Sunday review |

## Week 3 · Repeat without frills

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| 15 to 20 | Trade only in your window, only with a setup from the plan. Journal on the same day, emotional state before and after |
| 21 | Sunday review: adherence, losses with and without plan, one change |

## Week 4 · Look at yourself in the mirror

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| 22 to 27 | Carry on. Activate the stopping rules whenever it applies, with no exceptions (chapter 10) |
| 28 | Calculate the three numbers: adherence, win rate, average win ÷ average loss |
| 29 | Compare with the passing criteria (chapter 11) |
| 30 | Write a letter to yourself: what you learned, what you will keep, what you will change |

## Glossary

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Pip** | The unit of price movement; fourth decimal place on most pairs |
| **Lot** | The size of the position: 1.00 (standard), 0.10 (mini), 0.01 (micro) |
| **Spread** | The difference between the buy price (ask) and the sell price (bid); the cost of entry |
| **Swap** | The interest paid or received for holding a position open from one day to the next |
| **Stop-loss (SL)** | The price at which the trade closes by itself if it goes wrong |
| **Take-profit (TP)** | The price at which the trade closes by itself if it goes right |
| **Margin** | The money the broker sets aside to keep a position open |
| **Leverage** | Controlling a position bigger than your capital; increases gains and losses |
| **Margin call / stop out** | Warning and automatic closing when the margin is no longer enough |
| **Equity** | The balance plus or minus the result of open positions |
| **Drawdown** | The fall in the account from its highest point to its lowest |
| **Breakeven** | The point at which the trade neither gains nor loses |
| **Limit order** | Buys below or sells above the current price |
| **Stop order** | Buys above or sells below the current price, on a breakout |
| **Slippage** | The difference between the price requested and the price executed |
| **Timeframe** | The period of each candle: M15, H1, H4, D1 |
| **Support / resistance** | Zones where the price has stopped falling / rising |
| **Technical analysis** | Reading the price and the chart |
| **Fundamental analysis** | Reading the economy: interest rates, inflation, jobs |
| **CFD** | Contract for difference: you trade the price change without owning the asset |
| **Bid / Ask** | In MT5, the price at which you sell (left) and the price at which you buy (right) |
| **Points** | The unit MT5 uses for the spread; on five-decimal pairs, 10 points = 1 pip |
| **Volume** | The name of the lot in the MT5 order window |
| **Pending order** | An order that waits for a price: Buy/Sell Limit and Buy/Sell Stop |
| **MetaQuotes ID** | The code that links the PC terminal to the phone app so you can receive push alerts |
| **Tradays** | The economic calendar built into MetaTrader 5 |
