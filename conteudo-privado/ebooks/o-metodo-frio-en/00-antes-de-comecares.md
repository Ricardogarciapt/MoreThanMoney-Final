# Before you start

> The market does not hate you. It does not even know you exist. And that is exactly why you need a method.

There is a sentence I wish I had heard long before I opened my first chart: the market owes you nothing. It does not owe you a recovery, it does not owe you being right, it does not owe you the good week you think you deserve. It moves for millions of reasons that have nothing to do with you, and it moves in exactly the same way whether you are calm or whether your heart is pounding.

The only thing you control is what you do before you click.

This book is about that. It is called "The Cold Method" because trading does not reward the people who feel the most. It rewards the people who decide in advance, with written rules, and then stick to them when their stomach begs for the opposite. Cold does not mean heartless. It means the decision was made with a clear head, away from the screen, and that the moment of the click is only execution.

:::destaque
Hot is deciding during the trade. Cold is deciding before it, in writing, and in the moment only carrying it out.
:::

## Why I wrote this book

I made mistakes. Some of them were expensive, in money and in sleepless nights. I do not write them here to make a drama out of them. I write them because each one turned into a rule, and the rules are the part of this business that can actually be taught.

The first version of this e-book was called "Investing Without Emotions". It was a summary of a class: useful, but generic, and with a title that promised one thing (investing) and delivered another (Forex trading). This is the edition rebuilt from scratch. More direct, more practical, with MetaTrader 5 step by step through the chapters, real app screens and the classic mistakes put up front, so that you do not have to pay for them yourself.

If you have read my other book, "I Destroyed It All. And I'm Glad.", you know where I come from and why discipline is my obsession. This is its technical sibling. That one has the story and the mindset. This one is the workbench.

## Where I come from, in a few lines

I was born in Porto and adopted at 17 months by a humble family. Nine of us grew up in a century-old house with four or five rooms. Food was shared, hygiene was whatever was possible, and I watched people I loved get sick and go because of a poverty nobody chooses. It was only around the age of eight that I started calling them mum, grandma and sister. Those words were hard to say, and they stayed.

At 16 I asked my grandma Miquinhas for €25 to buy a pair of baggy hip-hop trousers. It was the fashion in Porto at the time and I wanted to be like everyone else. She looked at me and said: "Oh Ricardo, I can't anymore, everyone has already borrowed from me." The allowance I got for being adopted was feeding the whole house: there were sick people, there were unemployed people, and the money didn't stretch any further. That was the day I decided I was not going to die poor. A week later I was working and studying, and I moved into the house next door, which had a rent of €25. The same amount as the trousers.

:::cartao Portugal, for context
- **2003:** the national minimum wage was €356.60. It was the year I joined the Army.
- **2011 to 2014:** request for financial assistance, the troika programme and the minimum wage frozen at €485. In the public sector there were cuts to salaries and allowances.
- **2013:** unemployment went past 17%. Among young people, more than one in three had no job.
- **2021 to 2023:** the pandemic, then inflation of close to 8% in 2022 and mortgage payments rising with Euribor.

Public figures (INE, Banco de Portugal), rounded. They are there so you can see the ground that many people in my generation tried to build something on.
:::

I don't tell this so that you feel sorry for me. I tell it because Forex attracts a lot of people from exactly that place: people who count what is left at the end of the month, people who already work hard and want a way out. They are the ones who need a cold method most, because they have the most to lose with a hot one. Throughout the book I will tell you, chapter by chapter, the pieces of my life that taught me each rule.

## What this book is (and what it is not)

It is a manual for people starting out in Forex who want to do it without burning out in the first few months. You will understand what you are trading, how much each trade costs, how to place an order in MetaTrader 5 with a stop and a target, how to work out the right size and how to build a plan that fits on one page.

It is not a shortcut. There is no "never loses" strategy in here, no screenshots of profits, no promises of returns. Anyone who promises you that in Forex is selling you a dream, and the dream is the most expensive thing you can buy in this market.

| What you will find | What you will not find |
|---|---|
| Written rules you can copy | "Secret strategy" |
| Accounts with illustrative numbers, explained | Student results or profit screenshots |
| MetaTrader 5 step by step | Signals to follow without understanding them |
| A 30-day plan on a demo account | Rushing you on to a real account |

## How to read this book

This book does not leave the practical part until the end. In chapter 1 you install MetaTrader 5 on a demo account, and from then on each chapter adds a new idea to the app screen where it applies. You learn the spread by looking at your Quotes, the lot size by looking at the "Volume" field, the chart with your finger on the chart. By the time you reach the order in chapter 6, you already know how to use every piece.

[[grafico:percurso-mt5]]

Each chapter has the same parts, always in the same order:

1. **One idea** that changes the way you see the market.
2. **The screen**: where that idea lives in MetaTrader, with real captures of the app.
3. **One classic mistake**, the one almost everybody makes at the start, with an explanation of what it costs.
4. **A piece of Marta's story**, an example character who starts from zero in chapter 1 and applies each rule, stumbles included, to the end of the book.
5. **A piece of my life**, from Porto to the paratroopers, to the Marines (Fuzileiros) and to the GNR (Portuguese National Guard), with the moment I learned that rule.
6. **One cold rule**, short, for you to stick up near your screen.
7. **One task for today** on your demo or in a notebook, which takes less than 30 minutes.

:::cartao The five cold rules of this book
- I decide beforehand. In the moment, I only execute.
- No order goes in without a stop-loss.
- The lot size comes from the risk on the account, not from how I feel.
- Two losses in a row: I close the screen for the day.
- Everything I do goes in the journal.
:::

Read it with your phone beside you and, from chapter 1 onwards, with MetaTrader 5 open on a demo account. Read it with a notebook beside you. This book was made to be scribbled on.

:::exercicio Before you turn the page
