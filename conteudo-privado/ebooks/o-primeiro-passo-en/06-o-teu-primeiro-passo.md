# Your first step

> A small step taken this week is worth more than a perfect plan for someday.

You made it to the end. If you did the exercises, you already know more about your money than most people I know. Now it's time to turn that into a step. Here's a seven-day plan, designed for people with little time and no experience.

## Seven days, fifteen minutes a day

| Day | What you do | Done |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | You add up the three boxes from your statement (chapter 2) | ☐ |
| 2 | You cut one subscription or habit from the choices box | ☐ |
| 3 | You set up the automatic transfer for payday | ☐ |
| 4 | You open a separate account for your emergency fund | ☐ |
| 5 | You choose a path from chapter 4 and write down why | ☐ |
| 6 | You join the MoreThanMoney community and ask your first question | ☐ |
| 7 | You watch a live or recorded class and note one rule | ☐ |

:::destaque
You don't have to do everything perfectly. You have to do everything. Day 7 done in a rush is worth more than Day 7 put off.
:::
