# Before you start

> You don't need to know everything. You need to know what the next step is.

If you opened this guide, I suspect something is already bothering you. Maybe it's the salary that lands on the 25th and is already spent by the 10th. Maybe it's the feeling that you work more and more and life isn't getting any better. Or maybe you're just curious, because everyone talks about investing and nobody has explained where to start.

Whatever the reason, this guide was written for you. It's short on purpose. You can read it in one evening, and by the end you'll know three things: where your money is today, what options exist to put it to work, and what the first concrete step is that you can take this week.

:::destaque
This guide doesn't promise you'll get rich. It promises clarity. And when it comes to money, clarity is worth more than any tip.
:::

## Who I am, in a few lines

My name is Ricardo Garcia. I was born in Porto and adopted at 17 months by a humble family, in a home where money never stretched to the end of the month. I spent fourteen years serving the country, in the Army, the Marine Corps and the GNR (Portugal's National Republican Guard). I learned there the thing I use most today: discipline. Doing what you agreed to do, even on the days you don't feel like it.

[[foto: rg-farda.jpg | Fourteen years in uniform. The discipline stayed. | polaroid]]

When I started in the markets, I did what almost everyone does. I looked for the shortcut. I thought I was smarter than the banks and than people who had been at it for years. My first account, 3,000 €, went down the drain. It wasn't the market that took that money. It was me, with no rules, no plan and on my own.

What changed everything wasn't some secret strategy. It was stopping doing things alone, surrounding myself with people who already had what I wanted, and writing down the rules before risking a single euro. That's how MoreThanMoney was born: a school of financial education and markets where you learn with method and in community.
