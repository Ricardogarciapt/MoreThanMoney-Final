# Your money on one sheet

> You can't change what you can't see.

Sara, 29, a nurse in Coimbra, swore she spent nothing. She didn't have a new car, didn't eat at expensive restaurants, bought her clothes in the sales. One Sunday, after a challenge from a colleague, she opened her banking app and highlighted everything she couldn't remember buying over the last 30 days. It came to 214 €. Coffees, food deliveries, two subscriptions she no longer used and a gym she hadn't set foot in since February.

It wasn't much money each time. It was a lot of money added together.

## Twenty minutes with your statement

Before we talk about investing, you need to know where your money is. Not so you feel guilty. So you make decisions with numbers in front of you, instead of impressions.

:::ecra Banking app · Last 30 days
In: 1,380.00 €
Out: 1,342.60 €
Left over: 37.40 €
Active subscriptions: 6
:::

Illustrative example. Yours will be different, which is exactly why you have to do it yourself.

Split everything that went out into three boxes:

| Box | What goes in | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Fixed** | What you pay every month, whatever happens | Rent or mortgage repayments, electricity, water, internet, travel pass |
| **Variable** | What changes from month to month | Groceries, fuel, pharmacy |
| **Choices** | What you could have not spent | Coffees, deliveries, subscriptions, impulse buys |

The third box is where your first investment lives. Not because you're a big spender, but because it's the only one you can change without asking anyone's permission.

## The first rule: the emergency fund

Before you think about markets, build an emergency fund. It's money kept in a separate account, which you don't touch, to cover three to six months of fixed costs. It's for a car breakdown, a month without work, a dentist bill. Without it, any surprise forces you to sell an investment at the worst moment, or to take out an expensive loan.

[[grafico:fundo-emergencia]]

:::cartao Right order for your money
- 1. Pay off expensive debts (credit cards, consumer loans).
- 2. Build an emergency fund of 3 to 6 months.
- 3. Only then, invest money you don't need.
:::

:::alerta Classic mistake · Investing your emergency fund
I've seen plenty of people put their emergency money into the markets. At the first surprise, they had to pull everything out, often at a loss. Emergency money isn't money to invest.
:::

## Pay yourself first

The simplest trick I know: on the day your salary arrives, transfer a fixed amount to another account straight away. Even 30 €. Even 10 €. People who wait until the end of the month to see what's left almost never have anything left.

:::nota
Today: set up an automatic transfer for the 26th of each month. Amount: enough to hurt a little, but not too much.
:::
