# How not to fall for promises

> Anyone who promises you guaranteed profit is selling you a dream. And the dream is the most expensive thing in this market.

Every week I get messages like these. "Ricardo, a guy on Instagram guarantees 10% a month, is it true?" "I was asked for 250 € to join a signals group that never loses." "I was told that if I bring in two friends, I get my money back." The answer is nearly always the same, and it's short: run.

## The warning signs

:::cartao If you see this, be suspicious
- Guaranteed or fixed monthly returns.
- Screenshots of profits without the full history, without the losses.
- Pressure: "today only", "last spots", "the price goes up tomorrow".
- You earn by bringing in people, not from what you learn or use.
- You're asked to send money to a personal account or to a "platform" nobody has heard of.
- You can't find out where the broker they use is registered.
:::

At MoreThanMoney we are against pyramid schemes. We don't pay people to recruit others, we don't promise returns and we don't hold anyone's money. The money of those who trade sits with a regulated broker, in the person's own name. What we sell is education, tools and community.

## The five questions to ask before paying for anything

1. **Where is the full history?** With losses, dates and every trade.
2. **Who holds the money?** It has to be a regulated entity, in your name.
3. **How do I get out?** Cancelling must be simple and in writing.
4. **What happens if I lose?** If the answer is "you don't lose", you have your answer.
5. **Can I learn to do this on my own?** A good school teaches you to fish; it doesn't keep you tied to the boat.

## The five mistakes beginners make

| Mistake | What to do instead |
|---|---|
| Risking money you need | Only invest after your emergency fund |
| Wanting to win it all back in one day | A loss is a loss. The next trade starts from zero |
| No risk management | Decide how much you risk before you enter |
| Skipping education | Learn on a demo account first |
| Doing everything alone | Have a community to ask |
