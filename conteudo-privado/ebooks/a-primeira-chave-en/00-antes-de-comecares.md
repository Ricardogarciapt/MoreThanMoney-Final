# Before you start

> Crypto does not need more noise. It needs someone who explains it without selling you anything.

The crypto industry runs on two things: your fear of missing out and your hurry. The adverts show cars, watches and screenshots of balances that nobody can verify. The "mentors" sell signals. The platforms reward whoever clicks the most. And almost nobody explains the basics to you, because the basics do not make money for the people selling.

This book exists to do the opposite. I am going to show you how things work underneath, in plain words, without asking you to take my word for anything. You will understand what a blockchain is, where to keep a key, how to spot a scam in five seconds and how to buy without being glued to the chart. Then you decide. That is the whole point: deciding with your head, not with your notifications.

:::cartao What the industry will not tell you
- Nobody knows where the bottom is. Not even the person selling you the course to find it.
- Anyone who promises you a certain profit is selling the promise, not the profit.
- Your key is worth more than any coin you buy with it.
- The boring method usually beats the exciting one.
- You can lose everything you put in there. Start from that.
:::

An honest note before we start: this is not financial advice or a recommendation to buy anything, and I am not promising you results. I do not know your accounts, your debts or your goals. I do know the market, the traps and the calmest route in.

## The risk is real, and it can be total

A cryptocurrency can fall a long way, and fast. It has happened to coins that looked solid, and some have simply stopped having users and stopped being worth anything. When I talk about the risk of loss, I mean losing everything you put in, not just part of it.

Think of a coffee machine you buy second-hand at a flea market. It might be a bargain, or it might have hidden rust. There is no manufacturer's warranty, no complaints book, and the shop may have closed the next day. With crypto it is similar, except the value can change several times in the same day.

:::alerta
Never invest money you need to pay the rent, the transport pass, the electricity, the food or your instalments. If you lose that money, your life should not stop because of it.
:::

## The rule that never bends

You will see this rule repeated throughout the book, on purpose: never invest what you cannot afford to lose. It sounds obvious, and it is the one most people break when they watch a chart go up. The fear of missing out, the famous FOMO, pushes us into more than we had planned, and by then it is too late.

Before you buy anything at all, ask yourself a simple question: if this money disappeared tomorrow, would I still be fine? If the answer is no, this is not the moment for that money. It may never be, and that is perfectly all right.

:::destaque
Investing in crypto means deciding with the money you have left over, never with the money you need.
:::

## How this book is organised

The book has twelve chapters. We start with the basics: what money is and why Bitcoin was born. Then comes the technology behind it, the map of the coins, the first purchase, keys and wallets, the defence against scams, the regular buying method, how to read the market, taxes in Portugal, perpetuals (which I am leaving to the end on purpose) and a thirty-day plan.

You do not have to read it all in one go. I do have one request, though: do not skip the security part, even if it seems dull. Almost every loss I see among beginners does not come from a badly read chart. It comes from a message from someone pretending to be support, a secret phrase asked for over the phone, or a website that is almost identical to the real one. Boring is better than expensive.

## What this book is not

It is not a get-rich course. It has no secret formulas. It will not tell you when to buy or sell. It does not guarantee returns, percentages or results. When numbers appear in examples, they are always **illustrative examples**, with round, made-up figures, so you can see the logic. They are not forecasts, nor anyone's performance data.

Before you go on, keep these three rules in your pocket. They are not complicated and you do not need to memorise them.

:::cartao Three rules for the whole book
- Money you need for living stays out of crypto.
- No legitimate party will ever ask you for your passwords, codes or recovery phrase.
- If you do not understand it, do not buy it. Go back to the chapter.
