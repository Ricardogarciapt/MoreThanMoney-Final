# A 30-day plan

> You do not have to know everything. Just take one step a day, in the right direction.

You have reached the end. Or rather, you have reached the start of the rest. This last chapter is a practical plan for the next thirty days. You do not have to follow it to the letter. You have to adapt it to your life, your time and your budget. The aim is not to start investing at any cost. It is to finish this month with more knowledge, more security and calmer decisions.

## How to use the plan

Each day has a short task, of twenty to forty minutes. If a day goes badly, do not start again from scratch: move on to the next one. If a task asks for money you do not have, swap it for study. The rule is always the same: never invest what you cannot afford to lose.

Tick off the days as you go. Not to impress anyone, but so you know where you are when you come back to the book the following week.

## Printable security checklist

Print this list and keep it in plain sight, away from screens, so you can read it before every operation.

:::cartao Before every operation
- [ ] The password for the crypto account is unique and strong.
- [ ] Two-step verification is active on the email and the platform.
- [ ] Website addresses are typed by hand or come from verified bookmarks.
- [ ] No password, code or seed phrase has been shared with anyone.
- [ ] The seed phrase is on paper or metal, and does not exist as a photo or in the cloud.
- [ ] I have confirmed the address, network and amount before tapping confirm.
- [ ] I am not in a hurry, nor acting out of fear of missing out.
- [ ] I have talked to a trusted person about this big decision.
- [ ] I have recorded the operation in the sheet on the same day.
- [ ] If someone contacted me first, I confirmed through an official channel that I chose myself.
:::

## A glossary of 30 terms

1. **Altcoin:** any cryptocurrency that is not Bitcoin.
2. **Leverage:** using borrowed money to control a position larger than your margin.
3. **Blockchain:** a shared record, organised into blocks linked to one another.
4. **Genesis block:** the first block of a blockchain.
5. **Cold wallet:** a wallet whose keys are kept offline.
6. **Hot wallet:** a wallet connected to the internet, convenient and more exposed.
7. **Private key:** the secret that allows you to spend the coins at an address.
8. **Public key:** the address you can share in order to receive.
9. **Smart contract:** a program that carries out rules automatically on a blockchain.
10. **Custody:** who controls the keys to your money.
11. **DCA:** buying a fixed amount at regular intervals.
12. **Bitcoin dominance:** the percentage of the total market value that belongs to Bitcoin.
13. **Lump sum:** investing everything at once.
14. **Exchange:** a platform for buying and selling crypto.
15. **Halving:** the cut in half of miners' reward, every 210,000 blocks on Bitcoin.
16. **Hash:** the digital fingerprint of a block, which links it to the previous one.
17. **KYC:** the customer identity verification process.
18. **Liquidation:** the automatic closing of a position when the margin runs out.
19. **MiCA:** the European regulation on crypto-assets, applicable to service providers since 30 December 2024.
20. **Memecoin:** a coin created mainly for attention and humour, with very high risk.
21. **Mining:** validating blocks through proof-of-work, which consumes energy.
22. **Limit order:** a purchase or sale only at the set price or better.
23. **Market order:** a purchase or sale at the price available at that moment.
24. **Perpetual:** a derivative contract with no expiry date.
25. **Phishing:** a scam that imitates a website or a message to steal credentials.
26. **Proof-of-stake:** validating blocks based on coins locked up as a guarantee.
27. **Seed phrase:** a list of 12 or 24 words that recovers all the keys of a wallet.
28. **Spread:** the difference between the buy price and the sell price.
29. **Stablecoin:** a coin that aims to hold a stable value, usually pegged to the dollar.
30. **Volatility:** how much a price swings over a given period.

## To go further

When you have finished the plan, read other authors. Not to find a shortcut, but to hear the same truth told in different ways. These are good starting points:

- **"Bitcoin", by António Vilaça Pacheco.** A Portuguese author explaining Bitcoin from the roots, with historical context. (In Portuguese.)
- **"The Bitcoin Standard", by Saifedean Ammous.** The history of money told on the way to Bitcoin. It has strong opinions: read it with a critical eye.
- **"Mastering Bitcoin", by Andreas M. Antonopoulos.** For when you want to understand the technical side properly.
- **The original Bitcoin whitepaper, by Satoshi Nakamoto.** It is nine pages long. Read it at least once, even if you do not understand everything.

:::nota
Be wary of any book, video or course that ends with "buy now".
Including mine.
:::

## Final warning

This book was written to give you a foundation, not certainties. No method guarantees a profit, and neither does any platform, room or influencer. What is real is the risk of loss, and it can be total. Invest only what you can afford to lose, keep your keys carefully, be wary of pressure and always ask for confirmation from someone who understands tax and personal finance.

This content is educational and is not financial advice. When you make decisions with real money, make your own assessment, calmly.

## In short

- Thirty days are enough to understand the basics and start safely, or to decide not to start.
- The security checklist is worth more than any chart analysis.
- The glossary is there to consult whenever a word confuses you.
- Never investing what you cannot afford to lose remains the rule.
- Returning to your starting self-assessment is the best way to see what has changed.
