# Step 3 · Growing without greed

> Grow slowly, based on results, not euphoria.

A good month always comes. And with it comes the temptation: double the capital, double the risk, open more assets. This is where many people hand back to the market everything they've learned. Growing is part of the plan, but it has rules, like everything else.

## The scaling rule

:::exercicio My scaling rule
I only increase the capital or the risk when ________________________________

(Example: three months in a row of following the plan on at least 90% of trades, with the journal up to date.)
:::

Notice that the rule is about following the plan, not about making money. One month of profit without following the plan is luck. Three months of following the plan is method.

## Protect what you've built

- **Withdraw a part regularly.** Freedom also means sleeping soundly.
- **Keep the emergency fund intact.** Never use it to top up the trading account.
- **Review the plan every 30 days.** Adjust based on the journal, not your mood.
