# Before you start

> A simple plan you stick to is worth more than a perfect plan that stays in a drawer.

Most people don't fail in the markets for lack of information. There are videos, books and courses enough for several lifetimes. They fail for lack of a simple, written plan they can actually follow on a tired Tuesday, after work, with the phone ringing.

This guide is that plan. It has three steps, in order:

1. **Educate.** Understand the game before risking a single euro.
2. **Create income.** Choose the way of taking part in the market that fits your life.
3. **Automate.** Put rules and tools to work for you, so you don't depend on willpower.

:::destaque
Golden rule of this guide: don't move on to the next step before completing the previous one. Skipping steps is building the roof before the walls.
:::

## Who it's for

It's for people who have already realised they want to put their money to work and don't know where to begin. You don't need experience. You need fifteen minutes a day, a pen and the willingness to be honest with yourself on the worksheets.

If you don't yet have an emergency fund, or you have expensive debts, start with the other guide in this collection, "The First Step". This plan assumes that part is already sorted.

## How to use this guide

Each step has two chapters. The first explains, the second gets you doing. The worksheets are inside the text: write on them, even if you're reading the PDF, or copy them into a notebook. A written plan gets carried out. A plan in your head gets bargained with every day.
