# Step 2 · Choose how to take part

> There's no single right way. There's the way that fits your life and that you can keep up.

With the first step done, you now know what risk, stop and R are, and you have a routine. Now you choose how you'll take part in the market. There are three ways, and each asks something different of you.

## The three ways

| Way | What you do | Time per day | For whom |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Trade with the tools** | You read the scanner, decide and execute | 15 to 30 min | Those who want to learn the skill |
| **Follow the house ideas** | You get the idea with entry, stop and targets, and decide whether to enter | 5 to 15 min | Those short on time who want to control each trade |
| **Copy a strategy** | You link your account and set the risk | Weekly review | Those who don't want to trade but want to follow along |

### Trade with the tools

MoreThanMoney's scanners mark trend, zones and signals on the TradingView chart and the house WebTrader. They don't decide for you. They save you time and help you avoid seeing things that aren't there. Each scanner has a free video bootcamp. If you want to start with one, "The MTM Scanners Guide" explains where to begin.

### Follow the house ideas

The strategies' ideas reach your phone with everything marked: entry, stop and targets. With Tap to Trade, you open the trade with one tap on your account, once you've confirmed the volume. You're still the one deciding.

### Copy a strategy

With MTM Auto, your account at a regulated broker mirrors the trades of a house strategy. The money always stays in your account, in your name. Before copying, open the strategy's public audit and look at the full history, losses included. Past results do not guarantee future results.

:::alerta Classic mistake · Picking the strategy that made the most last month
Last month is noise. Look at consecutive months, at the longest losing streak and at risk per trade. If you couldn't live through the strategy's worst phase, don't copy it.
:::
