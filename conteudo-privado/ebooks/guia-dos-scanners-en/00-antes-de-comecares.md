## What a scanner is, without the mystery

A scanner is an indicator that runs on the chart and does part of the repetitive work for you: it follows the trend, marks the turning points, draws levels. It saves you hours staring at candles and helps you avoid seeing things that aren't there. It doesn't know the future. It isn't always right. Used with risk management, it gives you a process, and a process is what beginners need most.
