# From signal to trade

> The lot size changes. The risk doesn't.

Video for this chapter: **GoldKiller bootcamp, video 2 · Signal, plan, management and routine.**

Reading the chart is half the work. The other half is deciding, and deciding well means always following the same process.

## The four confirmations

:::cartao Before accepting a reversal
- **Closed reversal:** the signal candle has closed and the label has stayed.
- **Clear space:** there's no recent top or bottom between the entry and Gain 50.
- **One hour in your favour:** the latest signal on the 1-hour chart points the same way.
- **Calculated risk:** the lot was calculated from the distance to Drawdown 50.
:::

If any of them fails, you wait for the next one. There's always another reversal.

## Entry and stop

The entry is the close of the signal candle, which is the centre line. In practice, you can enter at the open of the next candle, which is usually right next to it. Don't chase price: if it has already run far from the centre line, the stop ends up far away and the target ends up close, and the trade stops being worth it.

The stop sits at **Drawdown 50**. If price goes beyond it soon after the reversal, it's behaving differently from normal, and the idea no longer holds.

## Position size

The distance between the entry and Drawdown 50 changes from reversal to reversal. If you always use the same lot, your risk in money changes without you noticing. Do the opposite: decide the percentage of the account you accept losing (1% is a common rule) and calculate the lot from the stop. On the MoreThanMoney WebTrader you enter the risk as a percentage and the volume is calculated for you.

## The three targets

1. At **TP1 (Gain 50)**, take off part of the position.
2. Move the stop to entry. From here on, the trade can no longer lose money.
3. At **TP2 (Gain 75)**, take off another part.
4. The rest runs to **TP3 (Gain 100)**, or exits earlier if the opposite reversal appears.

## When not to enter

| Situation | Why |
|---|---|
| Reversals one after another | The market is sideways and no move has room |
| A recent top or bottom before Gain 50 | The first target sits behind an obstacle |
| The 1-hour chart says the opposite | You're trading against the current |
| The entry is already far from the centre line | The risk is too big for the target |
| Major news in the next 30 minutes | Price can jump over your stop |

:::historia Pedro, first week
Pedro, 27, a student in Porto, saw seven signals on his first day and wanted to enter all of them. They were reversals one after another in a sideways market. With the table above next to his screen, he stayed out of six. The seventh had room and an hour in its favour. It was the only one he placed on demo, and it was the first time he felt he was deciding rather than reacting.
:::
