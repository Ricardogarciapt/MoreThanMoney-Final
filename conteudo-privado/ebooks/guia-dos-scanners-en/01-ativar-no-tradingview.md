# Activating the GoldKiller on TradingView

> Ten minutes of setup saves you weeks of confusion.

The GoldKiller lives on TradingView, the most widely used charting platform in the world. The free TradingView account is enough to use the scanner. Access is by invitation: we grant access to your username, and the scanner appears in your list of indicators.

## Step by step

1. **Create a TradingView account**, if you don't have one, at tradingview.com. Choose a username you'll recognise, because that's what you'll give us.
2. **Request access** at morethanmoney.pt/gratis/goldkiller, with the coupon FREEBIE and your TradingView username, written exactly as it appears on your profile.
3. **Wait for confirmation.** The team activates access within 24 hours and you'll get an email when it's ready.
4. **Open a gold chart:** search for XAUUSD at the top of the screen.
5. **Add the indicator:** click Indicators, then the "Invite-only scripts" tab, and choose MTM Gold Killer.
6. **Choose the timeframe:** 15 minutes for trading, and open a second window on 1 hour to confirm.

:::alerta Classic mistake · Wrong username
Access is granted to the exact name. One extra letter, or one missing dot, and the scanner won't appear. Copy the name from your TradingView profile rather than typing it from memory.
:::

## The house settings

Don't change the settings in the first few weeks. The levels you'll see (Gain and Drawdown 50, 75, 90 and 100) are the ones the alerts and the bootcamp use. If you change everything on day one, you won't be able to compare what you see with what's in the videos.

| Setting | House value |
|---|---|
| Asset | XAUUSD (gold) |
| Timeframe for trading | 15 minutes |
| Timeframe for confirming | 1 hour |
| Levels shown | 50, 75, 90 and 100 |

## A demo account alongside

To practise, you need a demo account to place trades on. You can use the MoreThanMoney WebTrader (morethanmoney.pt/webtrader), which has a demo account and shows the house scanners on the chart, or a MetaTrader 5 account with a broker. What matters is that it's a demo for these 30 days.
