# The MTM scanner map

> One tool at a time. Master one first, then get to know the others.

The GoldKiller is a good first scanner because it does one thing, on gold, with clear rules. But MoreThanMoney has other tools, each with its own style. All of them have a free video bootcamp on YouTube and a PDF guide. Watch the videos even if you don't use the tool: each strategy teaches a different way of looking at the chart.

:::scanner DEFY
foto: sc-defy.jpg
estilo: Exhaustion and key levels
ativos: Forex, gold, indices
tempo: 15 minutes to 1 hour
Exhaustion bands, key levels and star-rated confirmations. Each signal arrives with an entry, a warning line, a stop and three fixed targets on the candle. Nothing to work out yourself.
:::

:::scanner Golden Zone
foto: sc-golden-zone.jpg
estilo: Trend-following scalping
ativos: Gold (XAUUSD)
tempo: 5 minutes
Marks a zone and waits for price to come back to it. The entry is on the retest, not on the chase.
:::

:::scanner Momentum
foto: sc-momentum.jpg
estilo: Direction tracking
ativos: Forex, gold, indices, crypto
tempo: 15 minutes to 1 hour
Works with colours, with five confirmations worth one point each. The lilac boxes mark sideways markets, where you don't enter.
:::

:::scanner Aurum Flow
foto: sc-aurum-flow.jpg
estilo: Opening range breakout
ativos: Gold and crypto
tempo: Session opens
Marks the opening range and waits for the breakout. The stop sits on the opposite edge, so you know the risk before you enter.
:::

## And the WebTrader

If you'd rather not use TradingView, the MoreThanMoney WebTrader shows the house strategies directly on the chart, with the risk ticket in percentages and automatic management (Auto Break-Even, Trailing Stop and partial exits). It has its own bootcamp, with six videos: youtube.com/playlist?list=PLAXa8zbBvqFM.
