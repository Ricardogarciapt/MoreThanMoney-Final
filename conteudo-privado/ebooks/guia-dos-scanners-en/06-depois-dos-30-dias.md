# After the 30 days

> Decide with the journal in front of you, not the latest result.

You've reached the end of the month. Open the journal and answer three questions before you decide anything.

:::exercicio The 30-day review
1. How many trades did I make on demo? ________
2. How many did I follow in full (all four confirmations, the stop, the risk)? ________
3. What cost me the most: waiting, respecting the stop or managing the targets? ________________
:::

If you followed the plan on at least nine out of every ten trades, you have a process. If not, that's no bad thing: it's exactly what a demo is for. Repeat another month before you change tools.
