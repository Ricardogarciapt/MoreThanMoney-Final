# The routine and the journal

> A process followed on the dull days is worth more than a good hunch on the exciting ones.

The GoldKiller works for you all day. Your part fits into fifteen minutes.

## Fifteen minutes, five steps

[[grafico:rotina-15min]]

1. **Economic calendar:** is there any high-impact news today? Note the time.
2. **Gold on 15 minutes and 1 hour:** open both windows.
3. **Latest signal on both timeframes:** do they agree?
4. **Open orders:** which targets have been hit, and where the stop is.
5. **Journal:** update it.

If the scanner gives an alert outside your routine, go through the four-confirmation checklist before doing anything.

## The journal in R

After each trade, good or bad, log it. Measure the result in R: one R is what you risked. If you risked 10 € and exited at TP1 with one part and at TP2 with another, the result in R tells you how much that management was worth, without letting you get carried away by the figure in euros.

## Your 30-day plan

| Week | What you do |
|---|---|
| 1 | Activate the scanner, watch video 1, do the five-reversal exercise |
| 2 | Watch video 2, apply the four confirmations without trading |
| 3 | Trade on demo only the reversals that pass all four confirmations |
| 4 | Review the journal: how many trades followed the plan? Decide the next step |

[[grafico:sequencia-perdas]]

Even a good process has losing streaks. In 30 days you'll have some. Don't change the rules midway. Log them, and decide at the end, with the journal in front of you.
