# Módulo 7 — Your DCA with the MTM tools (≈16 min)
**Objetivo:** turn the DCA plan from the previous module into a fifteen-minute weekly routine, supported by the MoreThanMoney tools, without ever letting a tool make the decision for you. **No fim, o aluno consegue:** 1) read the DCA Inteligente and adjust the size of your top-up within a fixed ceiling; 2) use the MTM System app and the Portefólios to follow the markets; 3) confirm the day of the top-up with Momentum or DEFY on the daily and weekly charts; 4) complete the Friday ritual in fifteen minutes.

**Production note:** this module shows real app screens. Record the screens on the day of recording, with that day's data, and blur any balance or client name. Whenever a figure appears, it is an illustrative example and is said out loud.

## Aula 7.1 — DCA that is not dumb (≈3 min)
**Gancho (0:00–0:20):** "DCA has a reputation: it is the method for people who don't think. You always buy the same amount, on the same day, whatever happens. And what if I told you that you can think a little, without spoiling what is good about DCA?"

**[CENA 1]** Ruben in a medium shot, almost black background, details in gold. Text on screen: “Educational content. The risk of loss is real.”
**NARRAÇÃO:** In the previous module you wrote your plan: an amount, a fixed day, a platform. That plan is the most important thing in this bootcamp, and we are not going to touch it. What we will do in this lesson is give it a small steering wheel. Classic DCA always buys the same amount. Smart DCA always buys too, but it lets you adjust the size of each purchase depending on whether the market is more expensive or cheaper than usual. Notice the word “always”. Smart DCA never stops buying because of an opinion. It only changes the size.

**[CENA 2]** Animation: a price line with small gold circles on every Friday. At first they are all the same size; then, on the days when the price drops, the circles get bigger; on the days of euphoria, they get smaller.
**NARRAÇÃO:** The idea is old and it is common sense. When the price has dropped a lot in one day, your money buys more units. When the price has shot up, it buys fewer. If you top up a little more on the days of decline and a little less on the days of euphoria, your average price tends to become more balanced. Tends to. I don't guarantee anything, and nobody can guarantee anything to you. But it is a line of reasoning you can understand and check with pencil and paper.

**[CENA 3]** Screen of the MTM System app, Portefólios tab, “Análise DCA” panel. Slow zoom onto the labels: Forte Compra, Compra, Aguardar, Não Reforçar.
**NARRAÇÃO:** At MoreThanMoney this has a name: DCA Inteligente. Every day the system looks at the assets in the portfolios, crypto and ETFs, compares today's price with recent behaviour, and gives each one one of four labels. Forte Compra, which means strong buy, when there has been a sharp drop. Compra, which means buy, when there has been a relevant drop. Aguardar, which means wait, when everything is calm. And Não Reforçar, which means do not top up, when the price has risen a lot in a single day and it is best not to chase it.

**[CENA 4]** Animated table, gold on charcoal: “Forte Compra → up to 2× the base · Compra → 1.5× · Aguardar → half · Não Reforçar → zero on that asset”.
**NARRAÇÃO:** Each label comes with a suggested size, always relative to your base amount. In crypto, for example, a daily drop of eight percent or more gives Forte Compra and suggests up to double the base. A drop of four percent or more gives Compra, and suggests one and a half times the base. For ETFs the thresholds are lower, because they move less. Remember: these are suggestions from a system, for education. The decision is yours.

**[CENA 5]** Ruben is back, with a notebook. On screen the phrase appears: “The ceiling matters more than the label.”
**NARRAÇÃO:** And now the rule that makes this safe. Your monthly ceiling does not change. If your plan is one hundred euros a month, it is one hundred euros a month, whether there is a Forte Compra or not. How does that work? With a reserve pot. In the weeks when the label says Aguardar or Não Reforçar, the part you don't spend goes into the pot. In the weeks of Forte Compra, the extra top-up comes out of the pot. If the pot is empty, you buy the base and that's it. You never go and get money from outside the plan.

**[CENA 6]** Exercise sheet with four Fridays, one column “label”, another “purchase” and another “pot”.
**NARRAÇÃO:** Two-minute exercise. Imagine a base of twenty-five euros a week. On the first Friday the label is Aguardar: you buy twelve euros and fifty cents, and the twelve euros and fifty cents go into the pot. On the second, Não Reforçar: zero on that asset, twenty-five into the pot. On the third, Forte Compra: you buy fifty, twenty-five from the base and twenty-five from the pot. On the fourth, Compra. Do the sum yourself. An illustrative example, of course.

**Texto no ecrã:** smart DCA · never stops, only adjusts · fixed monthly ceiling · reserve pot
**Check question:** What happens in a Forte Compra week if the pot is empty? Answer: you only buy the base. The ceiling is not broken.
**Fecho / CTA da aula:** "In the next lesson, we open the app and you'll see where all of this is."

## Aula 7.3 — The Portefólios: learning by watching someone else (≈3 min)
**Gancho (0:00–0:20):** "The best way to learn to cook is not to read recipes. It is to stand in the kitchen next to someone. With DCA it is the same."

**[CENA 1]** Portefólios screen with the two house portfolios side by side: “Crypto” and “ETF”, with the evolution curve.
**NARRAÇÃO:** In the app there are two house portfolios: one crypto and one ETF. They are topped up every Friday, with fixed amounts per asset, and they are recorded in the app, purchase by purchase. You can see what was bought, when and at what price. It is an open DCA diary.

**[CENA 2]** Zoom into the portfolio's list of movements: Friday dates, asset, amount, reason “weekly DCA”.
**NARRAÇÃO:** Notice the dates. Friday, Friday, Friday. Weeks of good markets, weeks of bad markets, and the purchase is there. This is what I want you to see: the hard part of DCA is not the maths. It is carrying on buying when everyone around you says it is nonsense, and not buying too much when everyone says it is now or never.

**[CENA 3]** Animation: the portfolio split into coloured slices, with captions “one larger asset, some medium, some small”.
**NARRAÇÃO:** Also look at how the portfolio is divided. There is a larger weight on a few assets and smaller weights on others. I am not telling you to copy these assets. I am telling you to copy the idea: a portfolio has a structure, written down beforehand, and that structure does not change every week because of a headline.

**[CENA 4]** Ruben showing the notebook with a table: “asset · weight · amount per week”.
**NARRAÇÃO:** Now do yours. Write down the assets in your plan, the weight of each one and the amount per week. If you are starting out, less is more. One or two assets are perfectly enough. Remember the seven questions from module two before you put any name on this list.

**NARRAÇÃO:** Use the house portfolios as a mirror, not as a template. ## Aula 7.4 — Momentum on the big chart: choosing the size, not the day (≈3 min)
**Gancho (0:00–0:20):** "Momentum is a trading scanner. So what is it doing in a DCA module? It is acting as a traffic light."

**[CENA 1]** Screen recording of TradingView: daily Bitcoin chart with the Momentum scanner. Cloud, exponential average, volume panel and RSI.
**NARRAÇÃO:** You have already heard of Momentum, the MTM scanner that works with colours. Green for buy, red for sell. Traders use it on fifteen-minute or one-hour charts. We are going to use it differently: on the daily and weekly charts, the big charts, which show the underlying direction and not the noise of the day.

**[CENA 2]** Animation with five squares lighting up one by one: cloud, position of the cloud, exponential average, volume, RSI.
**NARRAÇÃO:** Momentum has five confirmations, and each one is worth one point. Is the cloud present and the right colour? Is the cloud on the correct side of the candles? Does the exponential average agree? Does the volume have the colour of the direction? And is the RSI crossing in the right zone? Five out of five is the most complete signal. Four out of five is still good. With fewer, Momentum is telling you it does not have a strong opinion.

**[CENA 3]** Three daily charts side by side, with captions: “green and in favour”, “strong red”, “lilac boxes”.
**NARRAÇÃO:** Now, how does this fit into DCA? With three simple readings, on your top-up day. First: a green daily chart, with four or five buy confirmations. The underlying trend is in your favour. You make the top-up that DCA Inteligente suggests. Second: a strong red daily chart, five out of five sell. The decline is under way. You buy the base, but you don't empty the pot yet. You wait for the selling pressure to ease. Third: lilac boxes on top of the candles, a sideways market. For a trader, this is where you don't enter. For someone doing DCA, it is an ordinary day. You buy the base.

**[CENA 4]** Weekly chart of the same asset, with a slow zoom out.
**NARRAÇÃO:** And the weekly chart? Use it once a month, to see the whole picture. If the weekly chart has turned to buy after a long decline, it is a good moment to review your plan and confirm that the pot is ready for the Forte Compra weeks. If the weekly chart is in strong sell, you change nothing. DCA was designed for exactly those phases.

**[CENA 5]** Ruben, close-up, with the phrase on screen: “The traffic light does not switch off the car.”
**NARRAÇÃO:** The golden rule: Momentum does not stop your DCA. Never. It helps you decide whether you use the pot or not, and how much. If you ever catch yourself saying “I'm not buying this week because it's red”, come back to this lesson. Red on the daily chart, for someone doing DCA, means base, not zero.

**[CENA 6]** Exercise sheet with three charts and three boxes to fill in: “reading”, “points”, “what I do”.
**NARRAÇÃO:** Exercise: open the daily chart of an asset in your plan with Momentum. Count the confirmations. Write down the reading, the points, and what you would do next Friday: base, base plus pot, or base without the pot. Do this for three Fridays in a row, without buying anything extra, just recording. You will learn more from this than from any video.

**Texto no ecrã:** daily and weekly chart · five confirmations · green: suggested top-up · strong red: base · lilac: base
**Check question:** The daily chart is red, five out of five sell. Do you buy? Answer: yes, the base. The pot stays saved.
**Fecho / CTA da aula:** "Next lesson: DEFY, for those who want a second opinion."

## Aula 7.5 — DEFY: when the price stretches too far (≈3 min)
**Gancho (0:00–0:20):** "There are days when the price falls so much that it looks like an elastic band stretched to its limit. DEFY was built to show you those days."

**[CENA 1]** Daily chart with DEFY: inner, middle and outer exhaustion bands around a smooth average, with the dashed projection.
**NARRAÇÃO:** DEFY is another MTM scanner. It draws exhaustion bands around a smooth average: an inner one, a middle one and an outer one. When the price stretches out to the outer band and turns with a strong candle, DEFY marks an idea. For a trader, it is a possible entry point. For us, who do DCA, it is something else: it is a sign that the price is stretched to the downside, far from its normal level.

**[CENA 2]** Zoom into a DEFY buy idea, with the stars above the candle.
**NARRAÇÃO:** DEFY also adds up confirmations. Is the price at a level where it has reacted before? Is the trend cloud on the same side? Is there a volume break? With three confirmations, one star appears. With more, two or three. And when everything lines up, the candle turns gold. The stars measure how many confirmations there are. They do not measure the certainty of the result, because that does not exist.

**[CENA 3]** Animated table: “DCA Inteligente on Forte Compra + DEFY with a buy idea on the daily → use the pot”.
**NARRAÇÃO:** How is it used in DCA? As a second opinion. If on your top-up day DCA Inteligente says Forte Compra, and DEFY has a buy idea on the daily chart, with stars, the two readings agree: the price is stretched and there has been a sharp drop. That is the kind of week where it makes sense to use the reserve pot. If only one of them agrees, you buy the base and a little more from the pot, if you want. If neither agrees, base only.

**[CENA 4]** Ruben drawing a small three-line decision table in the notebook.
**NARRAÇÃO:** Let's put this into a simple table, to stick next to the screen. Two readings in favour: base plus pot. One reading in favour: base plus half the pot, at most. None: base only. And above all of it, the monthly ceiling, which is not broken. Choose one tool, Momentum or DEFY. Using both at the same time, at the start, is too much noise.

**[CENA 5]** Warning on screen, large letters: “DEFY signals are for trading. In DCA, they only affect the size.”
**NARRAÇÃO:** Be careful of a temptation. DEFY draws entry, stop and targets, because it was built for trading. Using DEFY does not give your DCA a stop and targets. Your DCA is long term, and it stays that way. If one day you want to trade with DEFY, that is a different course, a different account and a different risk.

**[CENA 6]** Summary screen with the complete decision table.
**NARRAÇÃO:** Lesson summary: DEFY shows you when the price has stretched too far. In DCA, it only helps you decide whether to use the pot. In the next lesson we bring everything together into a Friday ritual that fits in fifteen minutes.

**Texto no ecrã:** exhaustion bands · stars = confirmations · two in favour: base + pot · one: base + half the pot · none: base
**Check question:** DEFY has a buy idea with three stars, but DCA Inteligente says Aguardar. How much do you buy? Answer: the base, and at most half the pot.
**Fecho / CTA da aula:** "Last lesson of the module: the Friday ritual."

## Aula 7.6 — The Friday ritual in 15 minutes (≈3 min)
**Gancho (0:00–0:20):** "Fifteen minutes a week. That is all your DCA needs from you. The rest of the week is for living."

**[CENA 1]** Animated clock with fifteen minutes split into five slices, each with an icon.
**NARRAÇÃO:** Let's bring everything together. The ritual has five steps and fits into fifteen minutes. Always do it on the same day and at the same time. I suggest Friday afternoon or the end of the day, but choose whatever works for you.

**[CENA 2]** Step 1 on screen: “Plan in front of you (1 min)”. Ruben opens the notebook at the page with the plan.
**NARRAÇÃO:** Step one, one minute: open your written plan. Base amount, assets, monthly ceiling and how much you have in the pot. Without this in front of you, nothing else gets done.

**[CENA 3]** Step 2: “DCA Inteligente (3 min)”. Portefólios → Análise DCA screen.
**NARRAÇÃO:** Step two, three minutes: open the Análise DCA panel in the app and write down the label of each asset in your plan. Forte Compra, Compra, Aguardar or Não Reforçar.

**[CENA 4]** Step 3: “Daily chart (4 min)”. TradingView with Momentum or DEFY.
**NARRAÇÃO:** Step three, four minutes: open the daily chart of each asset with your tool, Momentum or DEFY. Write down the reading: in favour, against or neutral.

**[CENA 5]** Steps 4 and 5: “Decide and buy (5 min)” and “Record (2 min)”. Record sheet with columns: date, asset, label, reading, purchase, pot.
**NARRAÇÃO:** Step four, five minutes: apply the decision table. Base, base plus half the pot, or base plus the pot, always within the ceiling. Make the purchase on your authorised platform. Step five, two minutes: record everything in one line of the sheet. Date, asset, label, reading, how much you bought and how much stayed in the pot. This sheet will serve you for taxes, which you will see in the next module, and for learning from yourself.

**[CENA 6]** Ruben closing the notebook. Final text: “Module 7 mission: three Fridays in a row, full ritual, recorded.” Underneath, the educational warning.
**NARRAÇÃO:** Module mission: do the ritual on three Fridays in a row and record the three lines. If you are not buying yet, do the ritual anyway and record what you would have done. That is how you train. And keep this: the MTM tools help you decide with more calm and more information. They do not decide for you, they do not guarantee anything, and the risk of loss is always yours. Educational content, not financial advice. See you in the last module.

**Texto no ecrã:** 1 plan · 2 label · 3 daily chart · 4 decide and buy · 5 record · 15 minutes
**Check question:** Which is the only step you never skip, even in a week with no purchase? Answer: recording.
