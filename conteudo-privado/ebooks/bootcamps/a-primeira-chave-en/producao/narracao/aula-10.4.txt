Nobody likes to think about this. But if your coins exist only in your head, when you are gone, they are missing for the people you love too. This has a solution, and it is simpler than it sounds.

Let us go to inheritance. This is the last security point of level two, and it is the one most people put off. The idea is simple: someone you trust needs to be able to understand what exists, where it is and how to access it, when you can no longer do so. You do not need to give them the seed, neither now nor ever. What you give them is a map. A map says that you have crypto, in which types of wallet and on which authorised platforms, what the copies are, where they are kept and whom to ask for help finding them. Written this way, the map protects your family and does not expose your coins.

Separate the information into three parts. The map: what you have and where it is, with no access words. The seed copies, which are the key, stay in the safe places from the previous lesson, and never in the same envelope as the map. And the contacts: who is the trusted lawyer or notary to ask how this works in your case, and who is the person who will read the map when it is needed. The golden rule is that no single part, on its own, gives access to your coins. Whoever reads the map does not have the key. Whoever has the key does not know what it opens. The two have to come together.

An honest note about the legal side. Inheritance rules vary from country to country, and they vary even more when the assets are digital. I am not going to tell you here what the law provides for your case, because that depends on your situation and on who will inherit. What I ask is that, before you consider your plan finished, you talk to a lawyer or a trusted notary. Take the map, without the access words, and ask how it should be drafted to be valid. And if you live or have assets in another country, ask about that one too. This is not legal advice. It is a warning not to leave the subject for later.

Now the level two mission, which brings together everything you did in modules seven to ten. First, your written DCA plan, with the fixed monthly ceiling and the reserve pot, on a single sheet. It has the base amount, the assets, the top-up day, the size rules with the MTM Smart DCA table and what happens to the money you do not spend. Second, the DCA alerts switched on in the MTM System app, in the settings. Third, the Friday ritual carried out four weeks in a row, with a record of each one in the notebook. If on a Friday you do not buy, you still do the ritual and record what you would have done. Four lines, one per week.

Why four weeks and not one? Because one week is a coincidence. Four weeks in a row are a habit beginning. And it is in habit that DCA works, not on the day it went well or badly. Notice also what I am not asking of you: I am not asking you to have got it right, or to have made a profit. I am asking that the process has happened four times, at the same time and on the same sheet. If one Friday is missed for a real reason, such as a trip or an illness, it is not a failure. You restart the count and carry on. What you cannot do is pretend the record exists when it does not.

Level two mission, in short: a written DCA plan with ceiling and pot, DCA alerts switched on, and four consecutive Fridays of recorded ritual. It is not measured by what the market did. It is measured by whether you did it. And inheritance comes in here too, in the form of a map, with the key kept separate. Remember the essentials: this is educational content, not financial advice. The risk of loss is real, and the MTM tools help you decide with information. They do not decide for you. Level three brings in perpetuals, which are a different game. Before we get there, the plan for this level has to be done.
