Welcome to level three. This level is optional and it is the most demanding. We are going to study the Aurum Flow Cripto strategy, a crypto perpetuals scanner optimized for the one-hour chart. It is not a money machine, nor a promise. It is a set of rules that you can read, test and, above all, refuse. Every line of this lesson is a rule. Before you go on, notice the warning on screen: the risk of loss is real, and it can be total. This is not a formality. It is the premise of everything that follows.

The first house rule: Aurum Flow only trades with the trend. If the market is clearly rising, it looks for buys. If it is clearly falling, it looks for sells. It sounds obvious, but many of a beginner's losses come from doing the opposite: buying a fall because it's already cheap, or selling a rise because it's already expensive. Being cheap or expensive says nothing about direction. It can get even cheaper tomorrow. The trend is the only thing that tells you which way the market is pushing.

Look at these two illustrative examples. On the left, someone buys into a strong drop, convinced the price will bounce back. The market keeps falling and the stop is hit. On the right, the entry happens after the price makes a new high and turns back up. Neither case comes with guarantees, and one nice example proves nothing. But the second is the kind of entry the method looks for, because the wind is blowing in the same direction as the boat. That is the difference we want you to train. Look at this carefully, because it is the basis of everything that follows.

Why the one-hour chart? Because it is a balance. The five-minute chart changes every moment and picks up noise. The daily shows the big picture, but gives few signals. The one-hour keeps the trend reading clean and gives signals at a reasonable frequency. That is why Aurum Flow was optimized for it. And you should know one thing: if you change the chart, you are using a different strategy, even if the name is the same. Don't treat the 1H as a technical detail. It is the foundation of everything.

An honest note before we move on. This strategy does not tell you how many trades you will win, nor how much you make on each one. I don't have that rate to give you, and no material in this course has it. If anyone sells you a signal with a win rate, be suspicious straight away. What the method gives you is discipline and a written plan. The rest you learn yourself, with your own markings, and that is exactly what we train in this module's mission, in a simulated account. There is no shortcut here, and that is why it is worth practising.
