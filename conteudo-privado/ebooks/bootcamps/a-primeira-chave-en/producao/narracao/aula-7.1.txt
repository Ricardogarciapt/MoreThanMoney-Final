DCA has a reputation: it is the method for people who don't think. You always buy the same amount, on the same day, whatever happens. And what if I told you that you can think a little, without spoiling what is good about DCA?

In the previous module you wrote your plan: an amount, a fixed day, a platform. That plan is the most important thing in this bootcamp, and we are not going to touch it. What we will do in this lesson is give it a small steering wheel. Classic DCA always buys the same amount. Smart DCA always buys too, but it lets you adjust the size of each purchase depending on whether the market is more expensive or cheaper than usual. Notice the word “always”. Smart DCA never stops buying because of an opinion. It only changes the size.

The idea is old and it is common sense. When the price has dropped a lot in one day, your money buys more units. When the price has shot up, it buys fewer. If you top up a little more on the days of decline and a little less on the days of euphoria, your average price tends to become more balanced. Tends to. I don't guarantee anything, and nobody can guarantee anything to you. But it is a line of reasoning you can understand and check with pencil and paper.

At MoreThanMoney this has a name: DCA Inteligente. Every day the system looks at the assets in the portfolios, crypto and ETFs, compares today's price with recent behaviour, and gives each one one of four labels. Forte Compra, which means strong buy, when there has been a sharp drop. Compra, which means buy, when there has been a relevant drop. Aguardar, which means wait, when everything is calm. And Não Reforçar, which means do not top up, when the price has risen a lot in a single day and it is best not to chase it.

Each label comes with a suggested size, always relative to your base amount. In crypto, for example, a daily drop of eight percent or more gives Forte Compra and suggests up to double the base. A drop of four percent or more gives Compra, and suggests one and a half times the base. For ETFs the thresholds are lower, because they move less. Remember: these are suggestions from a system, for education. The decision is yours.

And now the rule that makes this safe. Your monthly ceiling does not change. If your plan is one hundred euros a month, it is one hundred euros a month, whether there is a Forte Compra or not. How does that work? With a reserve pot. In the weeks when the label says Aguardar or Não Reforçar, the part you don't spend goes into the pot. In the weeks of Forte Compra, the extra top-up comes out of the pot. If the pot is empty, you buy the base and that's it. You never go and get money from outside the plan.

Two-minute exercise. Imagine a base of twenty-five euros a week. On the first Friday the label is Aguardar: you buy twelve euros and fifty cents, and the twelve euros and fifty cents go into the pot. On the second, Não Reforçar: zero on that asset, twenty-five into the pot. On the third, Forte Compra: you buy fifty, twenty-five from the base and twenty-five from the pot. On the fourth, Compra. Do the sum yourself. An illustrative example, of course.
