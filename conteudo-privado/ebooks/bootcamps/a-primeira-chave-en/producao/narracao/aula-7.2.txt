If you have to remember to go and check the market, you will forget. The trick is for the market to come to you, at the right time, and only with what matters.

Let's go to the app. Everything you see in this lesson is in the MTM System app, on your phone or on your computer. I won't show you balances, because they don't matter here. I'll show you three places: where the daily analysis appears, where the portfolios are, and where you turn on the alerts.

First place: the Análise DCA Inteligente channel. Every day the system posts a summary there, split into crypto and ETFs and stocks, with the assets that are in Forte Compra or Compra and a short reading of the day. You don't need to read this every day. You need to read it on your DCA day. If your day is Friday, you open Friday's post.

Second place: inside Portefólios, the Análise DCA panel. Here you see asset by asset: the price, the change, the label and a one-line explanation. This is the screen where you make your choice for the week. Look only at the assets that are in your plan. The others can be in Forte Compra all they like. They are not in your plan, so they are not your concern.

Third place: the alerts. Turn on the DCA alerts in the app settings. That way, when an asset enters a top-up zone, you get a notice. Careful, this is not an order to buy outside your day. It is information. The discipline of the fixed day still applies. The alert is there so that you reach Friday already knowing what is waiting for you.

An honest note. The app is a tool for following the market and for education. It does not buy for you, it does not keep your money, and it does not know what will happen tomorrow. You make the purchase yourself, on your authorised platform, as you learned in module three. The app helps you decide calmly and with information, which is already a lot.

Mission for this lesson: open the app, find the three places and tick each one. It takes five minutes. In the next lesson we will look at the portfolios, which is the best way I know to learn how to do DCA by watching someone else do it.
