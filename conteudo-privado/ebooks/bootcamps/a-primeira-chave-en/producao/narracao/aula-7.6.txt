Fifteen minutes a week. That is all your DCA needs from you. The rest of the week is for living.

Let's bring everything together. The ritual has five steps and fits into fifteen minutes. Always do it on the same day and at the same time. I suggest Friday afternoon or the end of the day, but choose whatever works for you.

Step one, one minute: open your written plan. Base amount, assets, monthly ceiling and how much you have in the pot. Without this in front of you, nothing else gets done.

Step two, three minutes: open the Análise DCA panel in the app and write down the label of each asset in your plan. Forte Compra, Compra, Aguardar or Não Reforçar.

Step three, four minutes: open the daily chart of each asset with your tool, Momentum or DEFY. Write down the reading: in favour, against or neutral.

Step four, five minutes: apply the decision table. Base, base plus half the pot, or base plus the pot, always within the ceiling. Make the purchase on your authorised platform. Step five, two minutes: record everything in one line of the sheet. Date, asset, label, reading, how much you bought and how much stayed in the pot. This sheet will serve you for taxes, which you will see in the next module, and for learning from yourself.

Module mission: do the ritual on three Fridays in a row and record the three lines. If you are not buying yet, do the ritual anyway and record what you would have done. That is how you train. And keep this: the MTM tools help you decide with more calm and more information. They do not decide for you, they do not guarantee anything, and the risk of loss is always yours.
