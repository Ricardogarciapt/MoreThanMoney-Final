We have reached the exam. Before it, one question only you can answer: what are you going to do with all of this, next week?

The final exam covers the fourteen modules. It asks about what you have learned, not about what the market will do, because nobody knows that. You will find questions about the key and security, about DCA and the pot, about the journal, about the 365-day rule and about reading risk. If you did the missions with paper, the exam is a calm review. If you skipped modules, this is the moment to go back to them. There is no trick. There is process, and the exam only asks for what the course has already asked of you along the way.

When you pass the exam, you receive the MTM certificate. It is worth being clear about what it is. It is a certificate of completion of this course. It is not a licence, it is not a regulated professional qualification, and it does not authorise you to provide financial advice to anyone. It shows that you completed the programme and did the missions. Use it with that honesty. If someone tells you that a certificate like this gives access to guaranteed returns, you are looking at the warning sign you already know from other modules. Notice that the certificate is the end of a study path, not the start of a career.

After the course, there is follow-up. One group session a month, for you to bring the questions that have come up in the ritual, the journal and the plan. And the course room, in the app's chat, where you can ask other students and clear up doubts about each module. One thing to watch: it is a space for learning, not for deciding for anyone. It is not a buy or sell recommendation, and nobody in the room knows your situation. If anyone asks you for money, data or your key, leave the conversation and report it.

Before you close the course, three things. First, the missions for the levels you decided to do, with the paper filled in. Second, the final exam, taken calmly and without rushing to finish. Third, the ninety-day plan signed and stored somewhere separate from your seed phrase. Don't mix the two, because whoever keeps the plan and the key in the same place has a security problem. This checklist is your proof that you reached the end with method. If any of these is not done, it is not the end of the world: go back to the corresponding module, do what is missing, and only then consider the course finished.

Now, the final warning, which is not optional. This course is educational content. It is not financial advice, it is not a recommendation to buy or sell any asset, and it knows nothing about your situation, your income, your debts or your goals. Crypto is a high-risk market. The risk of loss is real, and you can lose part or all of what you put in. Three rules to take with you. The key is yours, and no legitimate party will ask you for it: not support, not a technician, not someone friendly in a conversation. The example numbers are always illustrative, and be suspicious of anyone who promises you gains and of anyone who rushes you. And a written plan, with twenty-four hours of waiting, is worth more than any hunch.

Before closing, the final mission of the course: write your ninety-day plan on a sheet, with today's date, sign it, and store it somewhere safe, separate from your seed phrase. Then, answer the five review questions. Thank you for giving this course the time it asks for. Not everyone sits down to study this calmly, and that already says a lot about you. Remember: educational content, not financial advice, and the risk of loss is real. Look after your key. Until next time. Also reread the module 14 review about the spiral, because that is where temptation usually comes back.
