Moving from theory to practice is the step most people skip. Today we are not going to skip it. We are going to do it with paper and pen.

We are going to build a DCA plan, step by step. First, choose a monthly amount that you could lose without it ruining your life. It is not whatever happens to be left at the end of the month by chance. It is what remains after your fixed bills, an emergency fund and the taxes you already have to pay. Second, choose a fixed day, for example the day after payday. Third, choose the regulated platform where you will buy, and confirm that verification and authentication are in order. Fourth, write the exception rule: what you do in a month when it is not possible. Fifth, mark when you will review the plan, for example every three months. Remember: the plan is not a contract with the market. It is a contract with yourself.

One rule that is not negotiable: never use money you need for rent, your transport pass, water, electricity or the IRS, the Portuguese income tax. If a month goes wrong, you do not want to be forced to sell in order to pay for your home, and that is exactly when decisions turn bad. Start small. Ten or twenty euros a week is enough to learn the process, understand the fees and see how you feel when the price falls. That feeling is the most valuable part of the training. If you have doubts about the platform, ask before you buy, never after.

If you decide to join, remember that the group is there to help you learn, not to tell you what to buy. If it makes sense for you, take a look, with no rush and no pressure. The decision is yours.

Once you have written the plan, sign it with the date and keep it somewhere safe, separate from your seed phrase. Before the first purchase, confirm that the platform is verified, that two-factor authentication is active, and that you have the addresses saved in your bookmarks. And let 24 hours pass before you make the first purchase. That wait is the final test: if, the next day, you still want to, the plan is yours. If you no longer want to, good thing you waited. Do not rush just because the market seems to be in a good mood that day. Also write down what you will do if the price falls by half, so the rule is clear before the scare, not during it.

The module mission is this: write your DCA plan on paper, with the five points we covered, and sign it. Do not buy anything yet if you do not have the platform verified and two-factor authentication active. And always remember: this is educational content, not financial advice, and the risk of loss is real. You can lose what you put in. So the plan is your protection, and haste is the enemy. Keep this paper and reread it before every review.
