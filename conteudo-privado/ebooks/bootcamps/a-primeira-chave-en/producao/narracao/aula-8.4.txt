We have reached the end of the bootcamp. Before anything else, I need to tell you one thing clearly, out loud.

This bootcamp is educational content. It is not financial advice. It is not a recommendation to buy or sell any asset, and it knows nothing about your situation, your income, your debts or your goals. Crypto is a high-risk market. The risk of loss is real, and you can lose part or all of what you put in. Never invest what you cannot afford to lose. And do not make tax or investment decisions without speaking to a qualified professional, who will look at your full numbers and explain what applies to your case. If you have questions about tax, bring them in writing, and ask for a written answer.

If you take only three things from these weeks, let them be these. The first: the key is yours, and no legitimate person will ever ask you for it. Not support, not a technician, not someone who has been friendly to you for months in a chat. The second: the example numbers are always illustrative, and the oldest rule is still the best one. Be wary of anyone who promises to double your money and of anyone who rushes you. The third: a written plan and a twenty-four hour wait are worth more than any hunch, however confident it sounds. You don't need to memorise anything. You need to come back to these rules when the rush shows up. These three rules fit on a sticky note, and that is a virtue, not a flaw.
