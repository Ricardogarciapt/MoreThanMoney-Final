You already have several tools in your head. But Friday's question is only one: how much do I buy? Let us answer that question in order.

Let us put everything together into one method, with four steps, always in this order. First, the MTM Smart DCA label, which gives the starting point. Second, the daily readings: Momentum and DEFY, counting how many are in favour. Third, the context: sentiment and dominance, which are written down but do not change the number. Fourth, the monthly ceiling and the record. The order matters. If you start with the context, you begin building your story before you have looked at the chart, and that is exactly where decisions become emotional.

The label gives the maximum for the day. Strong Buy can go up to twice the base. Buy, one and a half times. Wait, half the base, and the rest stays in the pot. Do Not Add, zero for that asset that week, and the whole base goes into the pot. Do Not Add never uses the pot. Then the readings decide how much of the pot comes in. Two or more in favour: base plus pot, within what the label allows. One: base plus, at most, half the pot. None: only the base. In no row is the monthly ceiling exceeded.

Let us look at an illustrative example, with a base of twenty-five euros a week and a pot that starts with forty euros. Week one: Strong Buy, two readings in favour. You buy fifty: twenty-five from the base and twenty-five from the pot, and the pot falls to fifteen. Week two: Buy, one reading in favour. You buy twenty-five from the base and, at most, half the pot, seven euros and fifty cents. The pot stays at seven euros and fifty cents. Week three: Do Not Add. Zero for the asset, and the twenty-five from the base go into the pot, which rises to thirty-two euros and fifty cents. Week four: no reading in favour, so only the base is bought. The new money for the month was one hundred euros. What came out of the pot was money that was already saved.

This sentence is the most important one in the programme so far. In the example we have just seen, there was not a single week in which the plan stopped. There were weeks with a bigger purchase, one week with only the base, and one week in which the asset received no purchase, but the money went into the pot and remains part of the plan. There is one thing you never do: stop the plan because the chart looks ugly, or because everyone is euphoric. If your DCA stops, it is no longer a DCA. It has become a guess with a calendar.

Where is everything recorded? On the Friday ritual sheet you have used since module seven. Date, asset, label, Momentum and DEFY readings, one line of context with sentiment and dominance, how much you bought, how much went into the pot and how much came out of it. Without this record, the decision stays in memory, and memory tends to embellish what we did. With it, months from now you can see your own pattern: whether you buy more when you are euphoric, whether the pot always stays still, whether the label gives you more signals than the readings do. That is real learning.

Module mission: do the four Friday readings and record each one. On each Friday, follow the four steps: label, readings, context, ceiling and record. If you do not have money to buy, do the reading anyway and record what you would have done. The mission is the record, not the purchase. Four consecutive Fridays give you the first history of your own method. At the end, set aside five minutes and compare the first reading with the last. And remember: none of this guarantees results. It is education. The risk of loss is real, and it always stays with you. See you in module ten.
