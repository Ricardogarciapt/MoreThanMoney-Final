# The map of the method

> This chapter isn't for reading straight through. It's for keeping open next to the chart.

When I teach, there's a question that comes up in the first week, sometimes on the very first day: "Ricardo, where do I start?" And the honest answer is that it depends on where you are. Some people arrive without knowing what a pip is. Others have already burnt through two accounts and need something different: rules, not more theory.

That's why I made this map. It brings together in one place the order in which I teach, the tools we've built at MoreThanMoney and how I use them. It isn't the course. The course has lessons, live charts, corrected exercises and questions. This is the index of the route, so you know where you are and what the next step is.

:::alerta
Everything in this chapter is educational. The tools show information; they don't decide for you, they don't guarantee results and they don't eliminate risk. Train on a demo account first.
:::

## The four foundation modules

MTM Forex Academy always starts in the same place. Not with pretty charts, but with the ground: what the market is, how you get into it, which words you'll hear and how risk is measured. There are four modules, each with short numbered lessons, so you can go back to one when you forget something.

| Module | What you learn | Lessons |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Introduction | What Forex is, its history, what gets traded and who's in it | 101 to 104 |
| 2 · Tools | Broker, platform and how to choose both without falling into traps | 201 to 204 |
| 3 · Vocabulary | Candle, pip, long and short position, lot, entry, stop loss and take profit | 301 to 307 |
| 4 · Execution and risk | Orders, spreads, leverage, margin, risk management, risk-reward, styles, timeframes and sessions | 401 to 410 |

Throughout this book you've come across some dark boxes labelled "Course peek". They're pieces of these lessons. I've put in enough for you to grasp the idea, and left out what only works with the chart in front of you and someone correcting you.

:::curso Lesson 101 · What is Forex
It's the market where currencies are exchanged. Always in pairs: when you buy EUR/USD, you're buying euros and selling dollars at the same time.

- It has no central exchange. It works as a network, between banks, companies, funds and people like you.
- It opens Sunday evening (Lisboa time) and closes Friday evening. Five days, 24 hours a day.
- It's the largest financial market in the world by volume. That doesn't make it easier. It makes it more liquid.

In the course: you see how money has evolved, from direct barter to Bitcoin, and why that explains a lot about the price today.
:::

## The order in which I teach

There's an order, and it isn't by chance. I've seen too many people start from the end: they install an indicator, see a green arrow and press the button. Two weeks later they ask me why they lost. Usually they didn't know what a pip was worth on the pair they were trading.

1. **Foundations.** The four modules above. Without this, everything else is luck.
2. **Risk before entry.** Chapter 8 of this book, the vest. You know how much you risk before you know where you get in.
3. **One strategy, one asset, one chart.** You choose one tool and one market, and you stay with them long enough to know them.
4. **Journal.** Every trade recorded, with a screenshot and the reason you got in. Chapter 9 has the model.
5. **Weekly review.** This is where you really learn. The chart teaches little in the moment. It teaches a lot on Sunday, calmly.
6. **Only then, a real account.** And a small one. Or a simulated MTM Funded account, to train rules without your own money at stake.

:::nota In the first lesson I always say
Don't look for the best strategy.
Look for the one you can follow on a bad day.
:::

## The family of tools

Over the years we've built tools for TradingView. Each one came from a practical question from students: "how do I know this is a good zone?", "how do I know the market is sideways?", "how do I avoid getting in late?". They aren't money machines. They're ways of applying the same rule every time, without tiredness and without opinion.

Here's a portrait of each one, as I explain them in the first lesson. The full guide to each strategy, with all the confirmations, is in the members' area.

:::scanner Golden Zone
foto: sc-golden-zone.jpg
estilo: Scalping with the trend
ativos: Gold (XAUUSD)
tempo: 5 minutes
It doesn't send you chasing the price. When there's a signal, it marks a zone and waits for the price to come back to it. A yellow tick is a warning, not an entry: there's only a trade when the targets and the invalidation zone appear. A white flag means it's over. With the flag showing, you don't enter.
:::

:::scanner Momentum
foto: sc-momentum.jpg
estilo: Direction following
ativos: Forex, gold, indices, crypto
tempo: 15 min to 1 hour
It works with colours: green buys, red sells. It combines five pieces (cloud, position relative to the cloud, exponential average, volume and RSI) and each one is worth a point. The lilac boxes mark a sideways market, and a sideways market is where you don't enter.
:::

:::scanner Sniper Pro
foto: sc-sniper-pro.jpg
estilo: Intraday, momentum and reversal
ativos: Forex pairs
tempo: 15 minutes with 4 hours
The signal arrives with a score of +3 to +10, a stop and two targets. But the most important step is to look at the 4-hour chart: a sell is only taken with the larger downtrend. It's the tool that best teaches you to read two charts at the same time.
:::

:::scanner Supernova
foto: sc-supernova.jpg
estilo: Impulsive reversal
ativos: US30, NAS100, Bitcoin
tempo: Short charts, high-volume hours
Here you're almost always going against the larger trend, so the rules are tight. The star is just the first warning. The full signal needs three consecutive candles in the same direction and a close on the other side of the 50 average.
:::

:::scanner TrendShot
foto: sc-trendshot.jpg
estilo: Momentum scalping
ativos: US30, gold, Bitcoin
tempo: 1 and 5 minutes
The image I use in class is surfing: you wait for the wave, you get in, you ride it to the target and you get out. The signal comes from the crossing of the 20 and 50 averages, always in the direction of the larger trend and during the sessions with the most liquidity.
:::

There are two more that appear a lot in this book and in the classes: the GoldKiller Scanner, which uses the regression reading from chapter 7 on gold, and the MTM V3.4 Scanner. GoldKiller is a good first step precisely because it makes you think about averages and deviations, not arrows.

:::destaque
One tool, one market, one chart. For at least thirty days. Anyone who changes indicator every week never gets to know whether the problem was the indicator or themselves.
:::

## Which tool for which life

The right question isn't "which one is best?". It's "which one fits into my day?". A nurse working shifts isn't going to scalp for one minute. A mum who only has her lunch hour free needs a strategy she can decide on in a short time and that doesn't make her stare at the screen.

| If your life looks like this | Start looking at |
|---|---|
| You have 15 minutes in the morning and another 15 in the evening | Regression reading on the 1-hour or 4-hour chart (chapter 7) |
| You have your lunch hour, which in Lisboa coincides with London being open | Sniper Pro, confirmed on the larger chart |
| You have the afternoon free and can stay alert | Golden Zone or TrendShot, only during the London and New York overlap |
| You can only look at weekends | Daily and weekly charts; no scalping |

:::curso Lesson 408 · Trading styles
There are four styles, and none is better than the others:

- **Scalper:** seconds to minutes. Intense focus and quick decisions.
- **Day trader:** opens and closes within the same day. A structured routine.
- **Swing trader:** days to weeks. Patience on entry.
- **Position trader:** weeks to months. Large charts, few decisions.

In the course: you do a simple test of your day and how you react to pressure, and you leave the class with a chosen style and a written timetable.
:::

## The nuggets I give in the first week

These are the sentences I repeat so often that students already laugh when they hear them. They're here so you can cut them out.

:::cartao The room's rules
- The stop goes in before the entry. Always.
- If you don't know what a pip is worth on this pair, you don't enter.
- A sideways market isn't an opportunity. It's waiting.
- The larger chart sets the direction. The smaller one sets the timing.
- Two losses in a row and I close the day. I come back tomorrow.
- A signal without a plan is just an arrow.
- The journal isn't optional.
:::

## How to carry on from here

If you've got this far and what you've read has made sense, there are three ways to continue, and none of them requires haste:

1. **On your own, with this book.** Do the 30-day plan in the appendix. On paper and on a demo account.
2. **With the community.** At MoreThanMoney you have the foundation classes, the live classes and people walking the same path. Ask when you get lost.
3. **With the tools.** Once you have written rules, the scanners save you hours of screen time. Before that, they only give you arrows.

## In summary

- The foundations come first: four modules, from 101 to 410.
- Risk is learned before the entry.
- Choose a tool that fits into your life, not the one that looks fastest.
- One tool, one market, one chart, for thirty days.
- No tool decides for you.
